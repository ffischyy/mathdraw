PRIVACY POLICY — [PRODUCT NAME]                        Last updated: [DATE]

Short version: your camera image never leaves your computer, and the app does not
collect, send or sell any personal data.

CAMERA
The app reads frames from your webcam only while it is running, to find the position of
your hand.  Frames are processed in memory on your computer and discarded immediately.
The app never records, saves or uploads video or images of you.  ("Export graph with
camera" saves a picture only when you choose it, to a location you pick.)

NETWORK
The installed app makes no internet connections.  The only exceptions are links you
click yourself (Buy, website, e-mail support), which open in your browser or e-mail app.

DATA STORED ON YOUR COMPUTER
The app saves, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\[APP_ID]\ on your computer only:
  • your settings, last drawing and last equation (state.json);
  • your licence key, if you entered one;
  • diagnostic log files (logs\), which contain technical messages, not camera data.
Uninstalling leaves this folder so your licence survives upgrades; delete it to remove
everything.

PURCHASES
Payments are processed by [RESELLER, e.g. Lemon Squeezy / Paddle / Gumroad], which acts as
merchant of record.  Its privacy policy applies to the purchase: [LINK].  We receive your
name and e-mail address to deliver your licence key and provide support, and keep them
for [PERIOD] for that purpose only.

SUPPORT
If you e-mail us, we use your message (and any log file you choose to attach) only to help
you.

CHILDREN
The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
